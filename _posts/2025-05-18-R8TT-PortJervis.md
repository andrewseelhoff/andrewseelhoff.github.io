Ok, I need to provide some context here.

After all my friends and I graduated from undergrad, we found this group-podcasting app called Capuccino where you could record voice memos and every day, all the memos would get snipped together into a kinda podcast sorta thing. It was a great way for us to all stay in touch as we went our separate ways. 

A few of us are public transit and general train nerds, so we started a segment called "Rate this Train" where we, unsurprisingly, would take a train and rate our experience on it.

Well, Simmons Hall Friends, after long last I am bringing this back, at least in text form!

This is part of a bigger adventure to NY that I might detail in a full blog post. Or it may forever remain a mystery to whoever looks at this blog. Anywho...

### What is the Port Jervis Line?
This line is part of New York's MTA Commuter rail system... sorta. While starting and ending in NY, it actually passes through a few stations in New Jersey (this is where I boarded it!) and NJTransit operates it with under a contact with New York's Metro North Railroad.

### How was the Port Jervis Line? 
It was quite nice. It reminded me of Boston's MBTA commuter rail: not super fast, but comfy and going to where you need to go. The scenery in the countryside outside of NY was really pretty, and it was so cool to see the little towns dotting the way which got served by this train. It goes all the way to Pennsylvania's border with NY and sees around 600,000 passengers a year!

### Where did I take it? 
I took it to Tuxedo, NY (yep, that's where the suit got its name) on the way to Warwick, NY to see some family. I got to check out a tenth-generation American dairy farm which had nationally-ranked ice cream and yes, it lived up to the hype.

The whole region is stunning! The soil is especially dark and regionally known for its quality. I believe mostly onions were grown out here. We also went to a local cidery/distillery and had some awesome drinks. Would recommend if you ever find yourself just outside of New York but not quite upstate.

### Rating: 9/10

I don't have anything negative to say, except for the fact a fire near Penn Station meant I nearly missed all my connections. But, even then, MTA and LIRR pulled through!
