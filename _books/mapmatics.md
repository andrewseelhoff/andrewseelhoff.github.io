Mapmatics explores the intersection of mapping and mathematics across a wide range of topics, covering everything from great circle distances to the topology of a subway map to how districts get gerrymandered. It's a popsci-adjacent book which doesn't shy away from getting into some real mathematical topics, but they're presented in such an approachable way that it's still really engaging to read! The writing and narrative flow is great overall, making this a very easy book to recommend reading.
