Professional Summary

I’m an above-knee amputee, but also a researcher. I’m interested in the biomechanics of amputee gait and how we can improve prosthesis control methods to deliver real-world outcomes for people like me, with the broader goal of improving the state of powered and passive prosthetic devices at large.

Publications

  • 2025
    Adapting Biomimetic Kinematics for Controlling a Powered-Knee, Passive-Ankle Prosthesis Across Inclines
    IEEE Int Conf Rehab Robotics (ICORR)

    This conference paper introduces my adaptaitons to Kevin Best’s Hybrid Kinematic Impedance Control. My adaptaions adjust knee control commands to account for a passive ankle during inclined walking.

  • 2025
    Implementation and Validation of a Data-Driven Variable Impedance Controller on the Össur Power Knee
    IEEE Int Conf Rehab Robotics (ICORR)

    This paper details adaptations to Hybrid Kinematic Impedance cotrol in level ground and sit-stand tasks to enable use on the commercially-available Össur Power Knee.

  • 2025

    Journal of NeuroEngineering and Rehabilitation (JNER)

    In an n = 7 trial, we compare the Power Knee’s stock controller to our adapted HKIC paradigm as well as users’ take-home devices across a range of sitting, standing, and walking activities to determine whether results observed on prototype hardware can be observed on commercial devices.
